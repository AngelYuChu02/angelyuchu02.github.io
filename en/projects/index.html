Bone-Locked Wearable Ultrasound for Patellar Ligament Dynamics

Aug. 2025 - Present | IUS 2026 abstract, wearable musculoskeletal ultrasound, ligament biomechanics

  • Develop a bone-referenced wearable B-mode ultrasound workflow to estimate patellar ligament dynamics during cyclic knee extension.
  • Use a cortical bone ROI to suppress probe-motion artifacts and ligament ROIs to track axial tissue deformation across controlled cadence and loading conditions.
  • Extract per-cycle peak axial strain, maximum strain rate, timing, realized cadence, and normalized cross-correlation quality metrics.
  • Pilot results showed QC-passed cycles across all planned conditions with high tracking quality (median NCC > 0.997), supporting the feasibility of robust ligament monitoring during functional movement.
